# About Me
I’m Junlong Tong, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo, expected to graduate in June 2027. My research encompasses Efficient LLMs, Multimodal LLMs, and Time Series Modeling, with a primary focus on the advancement of **Streaming Multimodal LLMs**. Specifically, I investigate ultra-low latency streaming architectures, proactive response mechanisms, and highly efficient inference within dynamic multimodal environments. My long-term vision is to transcend the static processing paradigms of traditional AI by endowing foundational models with concurrent perception and reasoning capabilities. Ultimately, I aim to develop highly efficient, streaming-based architectures capable of processing continuous, real-world data in real time, thereby facilitating proactive and intelligent interactions.
